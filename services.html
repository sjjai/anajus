One-way Trips and Round Trips

ANAJUS Cabs offers convenient one-way and round-trip services across Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Kerala, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh. For drop trips, we ensure cost-effective travel by charging only for one way, eliminating unnecessary two-way fare.
